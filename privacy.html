1. Information We Collect #

a) Information you provide

  • Name, phone number, and email address when you register.
  • The details of emergency contacts you designate within the app to receive your SOS alerts.
  • Profile details, medical notes (optional), and any content you submit through support channels.

b) Background & Live Location data

  • Precise GPS Location: When you trigger an active SOS or intentionally share a Live Location session, we collect latitude, longitude, altitude, and accuracy data.
  • Foreground Service Tracking: To guarantee your safety, Ally uses a persistent background service to track your location even when the app is closed or your phone is locked during an active emergency.
  • Location is only transmitted to your designated contacts and relevant authorities during an active session.

c) Device & usage data

  • Device identifiers, operating system version, and app version to ensure robust background processing.
  • Crash logs and anonymised usage analytics to improve reliability during critical moments.

d) Sensor & hardware data

  • Microphone access may be used for optional voice-trigger detection; audio is processed locally on your device and is never stored on our servers.
  • Hardware button interactions (such as volume button presses for discreet SOS activation) are handled on-device.
settings

2. How We Use Your Information #

  • Provide, operate, and continuously maintain the reliability of the Ally safety platform.
  • Dispatch SOS alerts and stream real-time map data to your emergency contacts.
  • Personalise your experience, such as plotting relevant local resources (police, hospitals) on your map.
  • Ensure background tracking services remain alive during critical emergencies to protect you.
  • Communicate important updates, security notices, and platform announcements.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your location data for targeted advertising.

share

3. Sharing Your Information #

We share data only in the following limited circumstances designed to protect you:

  • Emergency contacts — your name, real-time location, and alert status are broadcasted during an active SOS or live-tracking event.
  • First responders & authorities — location and incident data when you or we contact emergency services on your behalf.
  • Service providers — trusted infrastructure partners (e.g., cloud hosting, open-source mapping tiles via MapLibre) bound by strict data-processing agreements.
  • Legal requirements — when required by applicable law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of any person.
my_location

4. Location Data & App Stores #

Ally's primary function is safety. To comply with strict App Store and Google Play guidelines regarding background location, we disclose the following:

  • "Allow all the time" Access: Ally requires background location permissions to ensure that if you trigger an SOS and put your phone in your pocket, your location continues to update seamlessly.
  • Foreground Service Notification: Whenever Ally is tracking your location in the background, a persistent notification will be visible on your device to ensure you are fully aware the tracking is active.
  • You can revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings, though doing so will disable the app's core emergency capabilities.
lock

5. Data Security #

We implement industry-standard safeguards including TLS encryption for all location telemetry in transit, and AES-256 encryption at rest. Because real-time tracking relies on isolated background threads, we ensure data transmitted from our services directly to your designated emergency contacts remains secure and tamper-proof.

schedule

6. Data Retention #

  • Account data is retained for as long as your account is active.
  • Location history is retained only for a maximum of 30 days to assist with potential legal or safety investigations, after which it is permanently deleted.
  • Anonymised analytics: up to 24 months.

7. Your Rights #

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Deletion — request complete erasure of your account and historical GPS data.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time (e.g., turning off background services).

To exercise any of these rights, email us at team@safetyally.app.

8. Children's Privacy #

Ally is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child without appropriate parental consent, please contact us immediately.

11. Account Deletion #

How to request deletion

  • Send an email to team@safetyally.app from the email address associated with the account you want to delete.
  • Use the subject line: REQUEST FOR ACCOUNT DELETION.

What gets deleted

We delete all data and activity associated with your Ally account, including:

  • Account details — name, email address, and phone number.
  • Emergency contacts and safety-circle data.
  • Location history and SOS event logs.

Timeline & retention

We will permanently delete all your personal data within 14 working days of receiving and verifying your request.
